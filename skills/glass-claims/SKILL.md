1. Find the customer's wording with search_policy_clauses ("glass", "windscreen").
2. Glass cover differs by wording:
   - Basic (BAS-4.1): glass is treated as a collision claim; the full collision deductible applies.
   - Standard (STD-4.1, STD-4.2): a chip or crack repair has no deductible; a replacement has a
     $100 glass deductible.
   - Premium (PRM-4.1): repair or replacement has no deductible.
3. Check that the story explains glass damage. A low-speed knock rarely shatters a whole
   windscreen; a stone chip, an impact on that panel, or vandalism usually does.
4. Vandalism or theft is a different cover: on the basic wording it is not covered (BAS-5.1), so
   do not fast-track a "someone smashed my window" story there.
5. A broken lamp from a parking knock fits a low-speed collision; check the estimate is in line
   with one lamp.
6. Recommend ADJUSTER_REVIEW with an open question when the story does not explain the glass
   damage or the cause could be vandalism; otherwise the usual checks apply.
7. Cite the glass clause you relied on.
