1. Search the customer's wording for the exclusion the story raises:
   - deliveries, paid passengers, renting the car out: commercial use (*-8.5);
   - track days, racing, timed events: racing (*-8.1);
   - an unlicensed driver, alcohol or drugs: driver exclusion (*-8.4);
   - damage done on purpose: intentional damage (*-8.2);
   - reported more than 30 days later: reporting deadline (*-9.1).
2. Check the facts, not keywords. "I deliver my kids to school" is not commercial use; "racing to
   the shop" is not racing; a spectator at a race track is not racing. If the words appear but
   the facts do not fit an exclusion, say so and do not escalate for that reason.
3. Use intake facts when present: driving_for_work yes points to commercial use;
   driver_is_policyholder no means asking who drove and whether they were licensed.
4. When an exclusion may apply, recommend ADJUSTER_REVIEW, cite the clause from the customer's
   own wording, and ask the one question that would settle it.
5. Never say a claim is excluded or not covered; a claims handler decides.
6. Commercial use can be allowed by the schedule ("unless your schedule says so", STD-8.5,
   PRM-8.5); mention that the adjuster should check the schedule.
