# ClaimLens Basic Motor Policy (fictional)

This wording is fictional and written for the ClaimLens learning project. It is not a real policy.

## 1. Definitions

### BAS-1.1 Your vehicle
Your vehicle is the car described on your schedule, with its factory-fitted parts.
Accessories added later are not included.

### BAS-1.2 Schedule
The schedule is the page that shows your policy number, the covers you chose and your deductible.
If the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule decides.

## 2. Collision

### BAS-2.1 Collision cover is optional
Collision damage to your own vehicle is covered only if your schedule shows collision cover.
Without it, this policy covers only your liability to other people.

### BAS-2.2 What we pay for a collision
Where collision cover applies, we pay the reasonable cost of repair, less your deductible.
We may use recycled or non-original parts.

## 3. Deductible

### BAS-3.1 Collision deductible
You pay the deductible shown on your schedule for each collision claim.
The deductible is taken off the amount we pay.

## 4. Glass

### BAS-4.1 Glass damage
Windscreen and window damage is treated as a collision claim.
Your full collision deductible applies to both repair and replacement.

## 5. Theft and vandalism

### BAS-5.1 Theft and vandalism are not covered
This policy does not cover theft, attempted theft or vandalism.

## 6. Rental vehicle

### BAS-6.1 No rental vehicle
This policy does not pay for a rental car while your vehicle is being repaired.

## 7. Towing

### BAS-7.1 No towing
This policy does not pay towing costs.

## 8. Exclusions

### BAS-8.1 Racing and track use
We do not cover damage while your vehicle is used for racing, pace-making, speed testing or on a
race track.

### BAS-8.2 Intentional damage
We do not cover damage you cause on purpose, or damage caused with your consent.

### BAS-8.3 Wear and tear
We do not cover wear and tear, rust, mechanical breakdown or damage that existed before the
policy started.

### BAS-8.4 Unlicensed or impaired driver
We do not cover damage while your vehicle is driven by someone without a valid licence, or by
someone under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

### BAS-8.5 Commercial use
We do not cover damage while your vehicle is used for hire, delivery or carrying paying
passengers.

## 9. Making a claim

### BAS-9.1 Reporting deadline
Tell us about an accident as soon as you can, and no later than 30 days after it happens.
A late report may reduce or prevent payment.

### BAS-9.2 Photos and evidence
Send clear photos of all damage, including one of the whole vehicle and one of the licence plate.

## 10. Fraud

### BAS-10.1 False or exaggerated claims
If a claim is false or exaggerated, we will not pay it and may cancel the policy.
Suspected fraud is referred to our fraud team and may be reported to the authorities.
