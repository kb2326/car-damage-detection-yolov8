# ClaimLens Standard Motor Policy (fictional)

This wording is fictional and written for the ClaimLens learning project. It is not a real policy.

## 1. Definitions

### STD-1.1 Your vehicle
Your vehicle is the car described on your schedule, including its fitted parts and accessories.
A trailer or caravan is not part of your vehicle.

### STD-1.2 Schedule
The schedule is the page that shows your policy number, the covers you chose and your deductible.
If the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule decides.

## 2. Collision

### STD-2.1 Collision damage to your vehicle
We cover accidental damage to your vehicle caused by a collision with another vehicle or object.
We pay the reasonable cost of repair, less your deductible.

### STD-2.2 Repair or total loss
If repair would cost more than 70% of the vehicle's market value, we may treat it as a total loss.
In that case we pay the market value just before the accident, less your deductible.

## 3. Deductible

### STD-3.1 Collision deductible
You pay the deductible shown on your schedule for each collision claim.
The deductible is taken off the amount we pay.

## 4. Glass

### STD-4.1 Windscreen and window repair
Repairing a chip or crack in the windscreen or a window has no deductible.
Use an approved glass repairer where possible.

### STD-4.2 Windscreen replacement
If the glass must be replaced, a glass deductible of $100 applies.
Replacement glass must meet the manufacturer's specification.

## 5. Theft and vandalism

### STD-5.1 Theft and attempted theft
We cover loss of or damage to your vehicle caused by theft or attempted theft.
You must report a theft to the police and give us the report number.

### STD-5.2 Vandalism
We cover malicious damage caused by someone else. Your collision deductible applies.

## 6. Rental vehicle

### STD-6.1 Rental vehicle after a covered loss
If your vehicle cannot be driven after a covered collision or theft, we pay for a rental car.
Cover is for up to 10 days and up to $40 per day.

## 7. Towing

### STD-7.1 Towing after an accident
We pay up to $100 to tow your vehicle to the nearest approved repairer after a covered accident.

## 8. Exclusions

### STD-8.1 Racing and track use
We do not cover damage while your vehicle is used for racing, pace-making, speed testing or on a
race track.

### STD-8.2 Intentional damage
We do not cover damage you cause on purpose, or damage caused with your consent.

### STD-8.3 Wear and tear
We do not cover wear and tear, rust, mechanical breakdown or damage that existed before the
policy started.

### STD-8.4 Unlicensed or impaired driver
We do not cover damage while your vehicle is driven by someone without a valid licence, or by
someone under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

### STD-8.5 Commercial use
We do not cover damage while your vehicle is used for hire, delivery or carrying paying
passengers, unless your schedule says so.

## 9. Making a claim

### STD-9.1 Reporting deadline
Tell us about an accident or loss as soon as you can, and no later than 30 days after it happens.
A late report may reduce or prevent payment.

### STD-9.2 Photos and evidence
Send clear photos of all damage, including one of the whole vehicle and one of the licence plate.
We may ask for more photos or an inspection.

### STD-9.3 Police report
A police report is required for theft, vandalism and any accident where someone is injured.

## 10. Fraud

### STD-10.1 False or exaggerated claims
If a claim is false or exaggerated, we will not pay it and may cancel the policy.
Suspected fraud is referred to our fraud team and may be reported to the authorities.
