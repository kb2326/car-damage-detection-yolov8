# ClaimLens Premium Motor Policy (fictional)

This wording is fictional and written for the ClaimLens learning project. It is not a real policy.

## 1. Definitions

### PRM-1.1 Your vehicle
Your vehicle is the car described on your schedule, including fitted parts, accessories and
permanently installed audio or navigation equipment.

### PRM-1.2 Schedule
The schedule is the page that shows your policy number, the covers you chose and your deductible.
If the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule decides.

## 2. Collision

### PRM-2.1 Collision damage to your vehicle
We cover accidental damage to your vehicle caused by a collision with another vehicle or object.
We pay the reasonable cost of repair with original manufacturer parts, less your deductible.

### PRM-2.2 New vehicle replacement
If your vehicle is less than two years old and is a total loss, we replace it with a new vehicle
of the same make and model.

## 3. Deductible

### PRM-3.1 Collision deductible
You pay the deductible shown on your schedule for each collision claim.
The deductible is waived when another identified driver is fully at fault.

## 4. Glass

### PRM-4.1 Windscreen and window repair or replacement
Repairing or replacing the windscreen or a window has no deductible.
A glass claim does not affect your no-claims discount.

## 5. Theft and vandalism

### PRM-5.1 Theft and attempted theft
We cover loss of or damage to your vehicle caused by theft or attempted theft, including stolen
keys. You must report a theft to the police and give us the report number.

### PRM-5.2 Vandalism
We cover malicious damage caused by someone else, with no deductible.

## 6. Rental vehicle

### PRM-6.1 Rental vehicle after a covered loss
If your vehicle cannot be driven after a covered collision or theft, we pay for a rental car.
Cover is for up to 30 days and up to $60 per day.

## 7. Towing

### PRM-7.1 Towing and roadside assistance
We pay for towing to an approved repairer within 100 miles, with no cost limit.
Roadside assistance for a flat tyre or a flat battery is included.

## 8. Exclusions

### PRM-8.1 Racing and track use
We do not cover damage while your vehicle is used for racing, pace-making, speed testing or on a
race track.

### PRM-8.2 Intentional damage
We do not cover damage you cause on purpose, or damage caused with your consent.

### PRM-8.3 Wear and tear
We do not cover wear and tear, rust, mechanical breakdown or damage that existed before the
policy started.

### PRM-8.4 Unlicensed or impaired driver
We do not cover damage while your vehicle is driven by someone without a valid licence, or by
someone under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

### PRM-8.5 Commercial use
We do not cover damage while your vehicle is used for hire, delivery or carrying paying
passengers, unless your schedule says so.

## 9. Making a claim

### PRM-9.1 Reporting deadline
Tell us about an accident or loss as soon as you can, and no later than 30 days after it happens.
A late report may reduce or prevent payment.

### PRM-9.2 Photos and evidence
Send clear photos of all damage, including one of the whole vehicle and one of the licence plate.
We may ask for more photos or an inspection.

### PRM-9.3 Police report
A police report is required for theft, vandalism and any accident where someone is injured.

## 10. Fraud

### PRM-10.1 False or exaggerated claims
If a claim is false or exaggerated, we will not pay it and may cancel the policy.
Suspected fraud is referred to our fraud team and may be reported to the authorities.
